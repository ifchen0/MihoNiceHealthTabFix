Small patches for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3328729902]Nice Health Tab[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2816826107]Miho[/url]. The Miho patches only apply when Miho is loaded.

[h2]Miho: operations list never settles[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] In RimWorld 1.6 the human surgeries (bionic parts, prosthetics, organ transplants and so on) are given to humans through their [i]recipeUsers[/i] list. Miho turns on Humanoid Alien Races' [i]humanRecipeImport[/i], which adds Miho to those lists, and the Miho race def also lists the same surgeries in its own [i]recipes[/i]. Vanilla [i]ThingDef.AllRecipes[/i] joins both sources without removing duplicates, so every one of these surgeries appears twice for Miho. In my game that was 23 duplicate surgeries.

Nice Health Tab identifies each row of its animated operations list by surgery and body part. Two rows with the same identity end up sharing one slot that is pulled toward two positions at once, so the list never slides into place. The vanilla Add bill menu also shows these surgeries twice.

[b]Fix:[/b] Once at startup, the surgery list of every Miho race def is deduplicated in place, keeping the first entry so the order does not change. Only entries that really appear twice are removed, so nothing is lost even if a Miho update changes its list. Other races are not touched.

[h2]Prosthetic arm drawn as wood[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Nice Health Tab maps the vanilla [i]SimpleProstheticArm[/i] to its [i]WoodenPart[/i] image, but that arm is made of steel and components. The actual wooden part in vanilla is the wooden hand.

[b]Fix:[/b] An XML patch removes those image overrides, so the arm is drawn like other artificial parts such as the bionic arm.

[h2]Buttons hard to see[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] The inactive buttons around the body doll are drawn at 25% opacity until the mouse is over them, which is hard to see on the dark background.

[b]Fix:[/b] They are always drawn with their hover color. Active buttons are unchanged.

[h2]Missing ears, jaw and limb bones not shown on the doll[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] The body doll only colors parts that have their own image. A missing ear, jaw, tongue, humerus, radius, femur or tibia has none, so the head, arm or leg still looks intact and the loss only shows in the list next to the doll.

[b]Fix:[/b] A white cross is drawn beside the head, arm or leg when one of these parts is missing. The part keeps its injury or prosthesis color. Hovering the cross or the part lists the missing parts, and clicking the cross opens their surgery menu. No surgery targets a limb bone, so for those the menu of the leg or shoulder opens instead.

[h2]Badly hurt parts shown as missing[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Nice Health Tab computes part health with its own copy of the vanilla calculation, so mods that change part health are ignored. With [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2260055393]Just A Flesh Wound[/url], a part that takes lethal damage is kept at 1 HP and can still heal, and the vanilla health tab shows it that way, but Nice Health Tab still sees 0 and draws the part as missing (not drawn at all) on the doll.

[b]Fix:[/b] When Nice Health Tab gets 0 health for a part that has not actually been removed, the game's own part health is used instead, so the part is drawn as seriously damaged. Parts that really are missing are unchanged. Without such a mod the result is the same as before.

[h2]Miho: missile turret error on load[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Miho's missile turret gun ([i]Miho_BuildingWeapon_Missile[/i]) lists [i]CompEquippable[/i] in its comps, but its parent [i]BaseWeapon[/i] already provides it, so the gun ends up with two. Both save the same verb, and loading a save logs [i]Exception registering Verse.Verb_Shoot ... An item with the same key has already been added[/i] once per missile turret.

[b]Fix:[/b] An XML patch removes the extra copy; the inherited one stays. Existing saves load fine and are cleaned up the next time they are saved.

[h2]Long range turret warning[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Enemy AI marks the area around player turrets to avoid, using the turret range plus 4 cells. For turrets with very long range, such as Miho's missile turret (range 200), that is beyond the largest circle the game has precomputed (radius about 80), so the log shows [i]Not enough squares to get to radius 204. Max is 79.81228[/i] for each such turret whenever the area is rebuilt (for example on every load).

[b]Fix:[/b] Vanilla already falls back to the largest circle in that case; the same value is now returned without logging the error. Behavior is unchanged. Works for any long range turret, not just Miho's.

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save. Nothing is saved.
[*]Writes one line to the log for each Miho race def that had duplicates, e.g. [i][NiceHealthTabPatch] Removed 23 duplicate recipes from Alien_Miho.[/i]
[*]Requires Harmony and Nice Health Tab. Miho is optional; load after it if you use it.
[/list]
