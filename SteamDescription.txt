Fixes the operations list in [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3328729902]Nice Health Tab[/url] not sliding into place when a [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2816826107]Miho[/url] pawn is selected.

[h2]Every surgery listed twice[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] In RimWorld 1.6 the human surgeries (bionic parts, prosthetics, organ transplants and so on) are given to humans through their [i]recipeUsers[/i] list. Miho turns on Humanoid Alien Races' [i]humanRecipeImport[/i], which adds Miho to those lists, and the Miho race def also lists the same surgeries in its own [i]recipes[/i]. Vanilla [i]ThingDef.AllRecipes[/i] joins both sources without removing duplicates, so every one of these surgeries appears twice for Miho. In my game that was 23 duplicate surgeries.

Nice Health Tab identifies each row of its animated operations list by surgery and body part. Two rows with the same identity end up sharing one slot that is pulled toward two positions at once, so the list never settles. The vanilla Add bill menu also shows these surgeries twice.

[b]Fix:[/b] Once at startup, the surgery list of every Miho race def is deduplicated in place, keeping the first entry so the order does not change. Other races are not touched. No Harmony patches.

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save. Nothing is saved.
[*]Writes one line to the log for each Miho race def that had duplicates, e.g. [i][MihoNiceHealthTabFix] Removed 23 duplicate recipes from Alien_Miho.[/i]
[*]Load after Miho and Nice Health Tab.
[/list]
