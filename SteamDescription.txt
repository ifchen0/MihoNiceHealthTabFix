Small fixes for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3328729902]Nice Health Tab[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2816826107]Miho[/url]. Both are optional: each part only applies when its mod is loaded, so the Miho fixes also work without Nice Health Tab.

[h2]Nice Health Tab[/h2][list]
[*][b]Prosthetic arm:[/b] the vanilla simple prosthetic arm is no longer drawn as wood.
[*][b]Buttons:[/b] inactive buttons around the doll are no longer faded until hovered.
[*][b]Missing small parts:[/b] a white cross beside the head, arm or leg shows a missing ear, jaw, tongue or limb bone; hover lists them, click opens their surgery menu.
[*][b]Badly hurt parts:[/b] parts kept at 1 HP by mods such as [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2260055393]Just A Flesh Wound[/url] are no longer drawn as missing.
[*][b]Part list:[/b] right-clicking a part in the list opens its surgery menu.
[*][b]Alien hair, ears and tails (experimental):[/b] Humanoid Alien Races addons (Miho, Ratkin, Weazel...) are drawn on the doll. On by default; can be turned off in Nice Health Tab's settings (Common tab).
[*][b]Gene parts on 1.7:[/b] gene doll parts such as the Yttakin tail show again.
[*][b]Child doll:[/b] follows the pawn's life stage instead of age under 15, so teens and fast-growing races get the adult doll.
[/list]

[h2]Miho[/h2][list]
[*][b]Operations list:[/b] removes the duplicate surgeries that make the list jitter and show twice in the Add bill menu.
[*][b]Missile turret:[/b] fixes [i]Exception registering Verse.Verb_Shoot ... An item with the same key has already been added[/i] on load.
[*][b]Guided missiles:[/b] a missile that rolled a hit explodes on its target instead of short of it.
[*][b]Permits:[/b] removes the [i]Permit choice ready[/i] alert that can never be cleared.
[*][b]Quests:[/b] fixes hospitality quest text shown as [i]ERR:[/i] when a Miho Satrap is the asker. Quests made before the fix stay broken.
[/list]

[h2]Any mod[/h2][list]
[*][b]Long range turrets:[/b] fixes [i]Not enough squares to get to radius ... Max is 79.81228[/i].
[*][b]Favor traders:[/b] you can buy from traders that take royal favor (such as Miho's relic curators), paid from the negotiator's favor.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]Requires Harmony. Load after Nice Health Tab and Miho if you use them.
[/list]
